Why dental health matters

Oral assessment

Veterinary examination helps identify tartar, gum inflammation, pain, damaged teeth and other oral concerns.

Professional cleaning

When appropriate, professional scaling and cleaning can help control plaque and tartar accumulation.

Individual planning

Some dental cases may require additional diagnostics, procedures or facility-based treatment.

Follow-up care

The veterinary team can advise on practical home dental-care habits after assessment.
