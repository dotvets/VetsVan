Prepare early for travel

Destination requirements

Rules differ by country, airline, route and travel date, so requirements should be checked well before departure.

Health examination

A veterinary assessment may be required before issuing or supporting travel documentation.

Vaccines & identification

Depending on the destination, vaccination history, microchip details and timing can be important parts of the process.

Documentation timing

Some documents are valid only within specific time windows, so scheduling should match the intended travel date.
