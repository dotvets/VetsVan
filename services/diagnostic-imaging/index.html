How imaging supports diagnosis

Clinical context first

Imaging is selected based on symptoms, physical examination and the veterinarian’s clinical judgment.

Targeted assessment

Diagnostic imaging can help investigate selected musculoskeletal, thoracic, abdominal or other concerns where appropriate.

Referral when needed

Cases that need CT, advanced imaging, anesthesia, hospitalization or specialist intervention can be referred to a suitable veterinary facility.

Integrated care

Imaging findings are interpreted alongside examination and other diagnostics rather than as a stand-alone result.
